I'm Andrew, and I work in automation using n8n. My job is to take any repetitive or time-consuming process — receiving data, tracking information, sending alerts — and turn it into a workflow that runs on its own, no manual intervention needed.

I started with the fundamentals of automation, and now I build workflows with real logic behind them: connecting external APIs, conditional branching, and real-time data intake (Webhooks), wired up to services like Telegram and whatever else a client needs.
